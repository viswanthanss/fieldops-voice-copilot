================================================================================
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: COMPRESSOR EMERGENCY RESTART (CP-200 SERIES)
Document ID: SOP-COMP-001 | Revision: 2 | Effective Date: February 1, 2024
Authorizing Body: Plant Safety & Reliability Engineering, Site A
Target Equipment: CP-200, CP-201, CP-202 Industrial Reciprocating Compressors
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE & APPLICABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This SOP governs the controlled restart procedure following an automated safety
shutdown or emergency fault trip on any CP-200 series reciprocating compressor.
Under no circumstances should an emergency restart bypass safety interlocks.

2. PREREQUISITES & SAFETY CHECKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Verification:
    If maintenance work or mechanical inspection was performed, verify all LOTO
    locks and tags have been removed by the lead technician.
2.2 Ambient and Casing Temperature Check:
    For thermal trip codes (specifically E17 High Discharge Temperature), ensure
    a minimum 15-minute cool-down interval before attempting any restart sequence.
    Discharge manifold temperature must fall below 140°F (60°C).
2.3 Oil Level & Pressure Verification:
    Inspect sight glass on crankcase. Level must be between 50% and 75% of sight
    glass height using ISO VG 100 synthetic reciprocating compressor lubricant.
2.4 Suction / Discharge Valves:
    Confirm suction isolation valve V-101 is fully open.
    Confirm discharge check valve CV-104 is functional and line is pressurized.

3. STEP-BY-STEP RESTART PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Inspect Fault Status on IW-CTRL-3000 HMI:
        Review the Active Alarm register. Note the trip code (e.g., E17, E08, E03).
        Acknowledge alarm on the touchscreen panel.

Step 2: Clear Mechanical Cause:
        Verify that the physical root cause of the shutdown has been resolved.
        (For E17: clean condenser fins, check cooling fan belt, check coolant).

Step 3: Reset Controller Fault Relay:
        Press and hold the yellow RESET button on the front panel for 3 seconds.
        Observe the FAULT LED transition from steady red to flashing amber, then off.

Step 4: Unloaded Pre-Start Sequence:
        Initiate UNLOADED START mode from the HMI.
        The suction unloader solenoids will energize, holding suction valves open.

Step 5: Motor Energization:
        Press START. Motor will crank and ramp to base speed (1,750 RPM) under
        zero head pressure.
        Run unloaded for exactly 300 seconds (5 minutes).
        Monitor oil pressure transducer: must reach >= 35 PSIG within 15 seconds.

Step 6: Step-Loading:
        Close unloader stage 1 at 5 minutes (50% load).
        Monitor discharge pressure and vibration for 2 minutes.
        Close unloader stage 2 at 7 minutes (100% full load).

Step 7: Post-Restart Verification:
        Record the following baseline readings in the maintenance log:
        - Discharge temperature (must stabilize between 160°F and 180°F)
        - Discharge pressure (standard operating: 125–175 PSIG)
        - Motor current (must be <= 68 Amperes on 460V 3-phase)
        - Oil differential pressure (must remain 35–50 PSID)

4. EMERGENCY ABORT CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Immediately press the Emergency Stop (E-Stop) pushbutton if:
- Crankcase or cylinder head produces knocking or abnormal metallic clatter.
- Discharge temperature increases at a rate exceeding 5°F per minute.
- Oil pressure drops below 25 PSIG during loaded run.
- Motor draw exceeds 75 Amperes full-load continuous.
