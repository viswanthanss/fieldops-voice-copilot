================================================================================
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: HIGH TEMPERATURE RESPONSE & INVESTIGATION (CP-200)
Document ID: SOP-COMP-002 | Revision: 3 | Effective Date: February 15, 2024
Target Alarm: Error E17 — High Discharge Temperature (> 185°F / 85°C)
Applicable Units: CP-200, CP-201, CP-202 (Compressors, Site A)
================================================================================

1. INCIDENT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Error E17 triggers automatically when thermocouple TT-201 at the cylinder head
discharge port registers a temperature exceeding 185°F (85°C) for more than
3 consecutive seconds. The IW-CTRL-3000 safety controller immediately trips
main contactor M1 to prevent thermal seizure and valve breakdown.

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS FOR FIELD TECHNICIAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Safety Isolation:
    - Wear thermal gloves and eye protection. Discharge piping will be extremely hot.
    - Confirm compressor motor has ceased rotation.
    - Do NOT manually depress contactors or force the unit to run while hot.

2.2 Thermal Dissipation Period:
    - Allow minimum 15 to 20 minutes for passive cooling.
    - Do NOT apply cold water or ice to hot cast-iron cylinder heads as thermal
      shock can crack the casting.

3. SYSTEMATIC ROOT-CAUSE DIAGNOSIS (PRIORITIZED CHECKLIST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Condenser / Aftercooler Airflow Inspection:
    - Inspect external condenser fin pack (Assembly CP200-RAD-01).
    - Check for airborne debris, dust matting, oil film accumulation, or blocked intake.
    - Inspect cooling fan drive belt (Part # IW-BLT-440B) for slippage or rupture.
    - Check cooling fan electric motor for rotation and proper directional flow.

Step 2: Coolant / Oil Cooler Inspection:
    - On CP-201 and CP-202 models with liquid-to-air aftercoolers, verify closed-loop
      coolant level in expansion reservoir.
    - Check oil cooler differential temperature (normal drop: 20°F to 30°F).
    - If oil cooler is warm to the touch across both inlet and outlet, thermal bypass
      valve TCV-204 may be stuck open.

Step 3: Discharge Valve Leakage (Blowby Analysis):
    - A cracked or carbonized discharge reed valve allows compressed hot gas to
      re-enter the cylinder during suction stroke, causing rapid heat accumulation.
    - Inspect valve plate assembly VP-200-A. Look for carbon deposits or fatigue cracks.
    - Replace valve reeds if seating surface shows discoloration or pitting.

Step 4: Suction Air Filter Restriction:
    - High compression ratios caused by choked suction filters increase discharge
      temperatures exponentially.
    - Inspect suction filter indicator (DP gauge). If red flag is visible, replace
      air filter element (Part # IW-FLT-200A).

4. CLEARING THE FAULT AND RETURNING TO SERVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Verify cylinder head temperature has dropped below 140°F (60°C).
- If fin pack was dirty, clean using compressed dry air or approved industrial coil wash.
- Clear error code E17 via HMI: Menu -> Diagnostics -> Clear Active Alarms.
- Follow SOP-COMP-001 for the mandatory 5-minute unloaded restart.
- Monitor temperature ramp rate. If discharge temp rises past 175°F within 10 minutes
  of loaded operation, shut down immediately and replace discharge valve assembly.
