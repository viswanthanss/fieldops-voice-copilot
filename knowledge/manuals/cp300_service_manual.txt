SECTION 1 — EQUIPMENT OVERVIEW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1  Product Family Description

The CP-300 Series industrial screw compressors represent IndustrialWorks Corporation's
next-generation variable-speed compression platform. Unlike the fixed-speed CP-200
Series (reciprocating), the CP-300 Series uses twin-helical screw rotors driven by
a Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) for precise speed and capacity control. This manual
covers the following models:

  CP-300   — Single-stage screw, 100 HP, 150 PSIG max, VFD-driven, air-cooled
  CP-301   — Single-stage screw, 150 HP, 175 PSIG max, VFD-driven, water-cooled
  CP-302   — Two-stage screw, 200 HP, 200 PSIG max, dual VFD, water-cooled

The CP-300 family uses a fundamentally different control architecture from the CP-200
series: the IW-CTRL-5000 controller, a 7-inch color touchscreen HMI, and an integrated
VFD monitoring subsystem. Many error codes overlap numerically with CP-200 codes but
carry ENTIRELY DIFFERENT meanings due to the VFD-centric design.

CRITICAL NOTICE TO SERVICE PERSONNEL:
The CP-300 error code table is INCOMPATIBLE with the CP-200 error code table.
Several code numbers (including E08, E17, E20) have completely different definitions
between the two product families. ALWAYS confirm the model series before referencing
error code documentation. Cross-referencing CP-200 documentation for CP-300
troubleshooting (or vice versa) WILL lead to incorrect diagnosis and may cause
equipment damage or personnel injury.

Applications: Variable-demand industrial air supply, process gas compression,
instrument air systems, and CNG compression. Variable-speed operation allows
the CP-300 to modulate between 30–100% capacity, resulting in 30–45% energy
savings versus fixed-speed units at part-load.

1.2  Key Technology Differences from CP-200 Series

  Feature                  CP-200 Series              CP-300 Series
  -----------------------  -------------------------  --------------------------
  Compression type         Reciprocating piston       Twin-screw rotary
  Speed control            Fixed speed (DOL start)    Variable (VFD, 30–60 Hz)
  Cooling                  Air-cooled, belt-fan        Air or water; direct-drive
  Lubrication              Splash/pressure gear pump  Injection-flooded oil system
  Control platform         IW-CTRL-3000               IW-CTRL-5000
  HMI                      4.3-in color screen        7-in color touchscreen
  E17 definition           HIGH DISCHARGE TEMP        MOTOR FREQUENCY FAULT (VFD)
  E08 definition           LOW SUCTION PRESSURE       VIBRATION FAULT
  Oil change interval      Every 500 hours            Every 750 hours
  Max ambient temp         115°F                      122°F

1.3  CP-300 Major Component Assemblies

  1.3.1  Screw Compression Element
         - Female/male rotor profile: 4+6 lobe asymmetric (GHH-Rand licensed profile)
         - Rotor material: ductile iron with titanium nitride coating
         - Rotor clearances: 0.002–0.004 in radial; maintained by precision bearings
         - Oil injection rate: 8–12 GPM at full load for cooling and sealing
         - Male rotor speed: 1,800–3,600 RPM (VFD controlled)

  1.3.2  Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) Assembly
         - VFD rating: 460V/3Ø/60Hz input; 0–480V/0–120Hz output
         - VFD technology: IGBT-based PWM, integrated DC bus choke
         - VFD cooling: dedicated internal fan; air-purged enclosure
         - Output frequency range: 15–75 Hz (30–150% of base 50 Hz)
         - Motor speed range: 900–4,500 RPM
         - The VFD continuously monitors output frequency and compares to commanded
           setpoint. Deviation exceeding ±2 Hz for more than 3 seconds triggers E17.

  1.3.3  Oil System
         - Oil sump capacity: 8.0 quarts (CP-300/301); 12.0 quarts (CP-302)
         - Oil type: IW-ScrewLube 32 (polyalphaolefin synthetic, ISO VG 32)
         - Oil injection temperature: 140–160°F optimal
         - Oil separator: coalescing type; oil carryover <3 ppm at rated conditions
         - Oil cooler: plate-and-frame heat exchanger (CP-301/302 water-cooled)
         - Thermostatic bypass valve maintains oil injection temperature

  1.3.4  Cooling System
         - CP-300: Air-cooled; direct-drive axial fan, no belt system
         - CP-301/302: Water-cooled; plate-and-frame oil cooler and aftercooler
         - Cooling water requirements: 25–40 GPM, inlet temp <85°F, 30–100 PSIG
         - The CP-300 has NO belt-driven cooling fan — this is a common confusion
           with CP-200 service technicians unfamiliar with the CP-300 platform

1.4  Nameplate and Identification

CP-300 series nameplates are located on the front door panel. Serial numbers
follow the format: CP3-YYYYMM-XXXX. Units with serial numbers below CP3-202305-0500
(i.e., serial sequence < 30500) may require VFD firmware update — see Bulletin
BULL-2023-008 for details regarding spurious E17 faults on earlier units.

SECTION 2 — SAFETY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1  VFD-Specific Safety Warnings

DANGER: The VFD maintains a DC bus voltage up to 750V for up to 10 MINUTES
after power is removed. NEVER open the VFD enclosure until the DC bus indicator
light is extinguished and at least 10 minutes have elapsed after power-off.
Failure to observe this warning can result in electrocution and death.

WARNING: The CP-300 VFD can output frequencies up to 75 Hz, causing the motor
to operate at up to 150% of its nameplate speed. At elevated speeds, centrifugal
forces on rotating components increase with the square of speed. Unauthorized
VFD parameter changes above the rated maximum frequency are strictly prohibited
and will void the warranty.

  - LOTO must include both the main power disconnect AND the VFD control power
    (separate 120V circuit for VFD logic board and cooling fans)
  - Verify ZERO voltage at VFD input terminals before any VFD service
  - Ensure proper VFD drive-end motor bearing insulation (shaft grounding ring
    required — installed at factory; do not remove)

2.2  General Safety

All general safety warnings from Section 2 of the CP-200 series manual apply.
In addition, note that the CP-300's high-speed screw element can store significant
kinetic energy at full speed. Allow rotor deceleration to complete (HMI shows
"STOPPED") before opening any compression-side covers.

SECTION 3 — INSTALLATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1  Site Requirements

  - Minimum clearance: 48 inches on VFD service side; 36 inches on all other sides
  - Floor load capacity: 4,500 lbs (CP-300), 6,000 lbs (CP-301), 8,500 lbs (CP-302)
  - Ventilation: minimum 30 air changes per hour; VFD enclosure purge air must
    be filtered (particle size <5 micron, humidity <85% non-condensing)
  - Electrical: 460V/3Ø/60Hz with <5% total harmonic distortion at supply bus
    (excessive THD degrades VFD reliability and may trigger spurious E17 faults)
  - Ground: dedicated equipment ground, <1Ω to earth (VFD requires low-impedance ground)

3.2  VFD Commissioning Parameters

  Upon initial installation, verify these VFD parameters before first start:
  (Access via IW-CTRL-5000 HMI → Settings → VFD Parameters)

  Parameter                 Factory Default   Range
  ------------------------  ----------------  ------------------
  Base frequency (P1.01)    60 Hz             50–60 Hz
  Maximum frequency (P1.02) 65 Hz             50–75 Hz
  Minimum frequency (P1.03) 20 Hz             10–30 Hz
  Accel time (P2.01)        15 seconds        5–60 seconds
  Decel time (P2.02)        20 seconds        5–60 seconds
  Current limit (P3.01)     110% FLA          100–130%
  Frequency deviation alarm  ±2 Hz            ±1–5 Hz (E17 threshold)

  WARNING: Do not modify VFD parameters without authorization from IndustrialWorks
  engineering. Incorrect parameters can cause E17 motor frequency faults, motor
  overheating, or mechanical damage to the screw element.

SECTION 4 — OPERATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1  Pre-Startup Checks — CP-300 Specific

  [ ] Verify oil level on sight glass (FULL mark, cold reading)
  [ ] Check oil condition — should be clear amber; milky or dark = change oil
  [ ] Confirm VFD cooling fans are operational (audible on power-up)
  [ ] Verify VFD DC bus indicator light is OFF from previous operation
  [ ] Confirm inlet filter restriction indicator is in GREEN zone
  [ ] Verify all VFD input and output cable connections are tight (annual torque check)
  [ ] Confirm that VFD firmware version is 3.2.1 or later (check HMI → About →
      VFD Firmware; units with older firmware are subject to spurious E17 faults
      per Bulletin BULL-2023-008)

4.2  Normal Start Sequence

  1. Power ON at main disconnect; wait 30 seconds for VFD boot sequence
  2. HMI displays "VFD READY — SYSTEM OK"
  3. Press START; VFD ramps to minimum frequency (20 Hz) in soft-start mode
  4. Over 15 seconds, VFD accelerates to 45 Hz (75% speed, no-load)
  5. Capacity controller engages; VFD modulates frequency to match demand
  6. At steady state, VFD output frequency varies between 30–65 Hz

4.3  Normal Operating Parameters — CP-300

  Parameter                   Normal Range        Warning           Fault (E-code)
  --------------------------  ------------------  ----------------  ----------------
  Discharge Pressure (PSIG)   100–150             >145              >155 (E07)
  Discharge Temperature (°F)  160–195             >200              >220 (E11*)
  VFD Output Frequency (Hz)   30–65               ±2 Hz deviation   ±2 Hz >3s (E17)
  Oil Injection Temp (°F)     140–160             >170              >185 (E11*)
  Motor Amps                  ≤FLA rating         >90% FLA          >110% FLA (E03)
  Vibration (mm/s RMS)        <2.5                >4.0              >7.1 (E08)

  * E11 on CP-300 is oil temperature; E17 is VFD frequency. Discharge temperature
    on CP-300 does NOT trigger E17 (unlike CP-200 where E17 = HIGH DISCHARGE TEMP).

SECTION 5 — MAINTENANCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1  Preventive Maintenance Schedule — CP-300

IMPORTANT: The CP-300 maintenance schedule differs significantly from CP-200.
The extended oil change interval (750 hours vs. 500 hours) is due to the
synthetic lubricant used in CP-300 units. Do NOT substitute mineral oil.

  Interval          Task
  ----------------  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  Every 8 hours     Check oil level; check for unusual noise; verify HMI shows no warnings
  Every 125 hours   Inspect inlet filter restriction indicator; replace if at RED
  Every 250 hours   VFD cooling fan filter replacement; inspect VFD cable torque
  Every 500 hours   Inlet air filter replacement; oil separator differential check
  Every 750 hours   FULL OIL CHANGE — IW-ScrewLube 32 synthetic only
  Every 1,500 hrs   Oil separator element replacement; oil filter replacement
  Every 3,000 hrs   Screw element inspection; bearing clearance measurement; belt N/A
  Every 6,000 hrs   Major overhaul: rotor set, bearings, seals

5.2  Oil Change Procedure — CP-300 (Every 750 Hours)

  Oil type: IW-ScrewLube 32 ONLY — do NOT use mineral-based compressor oil
  Capacity: 8.0 quarts (CP-300/301) | 12.0 quarts (CP-302)

  NOTE: The CP-300 oil system is under positive pressure during operation.
  NEVER attempt to drain oil while the unit is running or pressurized.

  1. Run unit for 5 minutes to warm oil to 150°F
  2. LOTO the unit; verify system pressure is ZERO (check HMI pressure display)
  3. Open oil drain valve (ball valve, bottom of oil sump)
  4. Allow to drain completely — allow 20 minutes for synthetic oil (more viscous
     when cold; warm drainage is much faster)
  5. Remove and replace oil filter (IW-OILF-CP3-01)
  6. Close drain valve; fill sump via fill port to FULL mark
  7. Run 3 minutes; recheck level; top off if necessary
  8. Verify oil injection temperature reaches 140°F within 5 minutes of operation

5.3  VFD Preventive Maintenance

  CAUTION: VFD maintenance requires qualified electricians or VFD technicians.

  Every 250 hours:
  - Replace VFD enclosure air filter (IW-VFDF-CP3-01)
  - Clean filter housing with dry brush; do NOT use compressed air inside VFD enclosure
  - Inspect filter: if filter is more than 50% loaded, reduce replacement interval

  Every 1,000 hours:
  - Torque-check all VFD input and output cable lugs (torque per VFD manual IW-VFD-5K)
  - Inspect DC bus capacitors for bulging, leakage, or discoloration
  - Verify VFD firmware version (should be 3.2.1 or later; update if needed)
  - Test VFD output frequency with calibrated power meter; compare to HMI reading

  Every 5 years or 20,000 hours:
  - Replace VFD DC bus capacitors (factory service only)
  - Replace VFD cooling fans

SECTION 6 — TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1  Troubleshooting Methodology — CP-300

Due to the VFD-centric design, CP-300 troubleshooting often involves VFD
parameter and electrical diagnostics in addition to mechanical checks. Always
start with HMI fault log (HMI → Faults → History) to see fault code, timestamp,
operating conditions at fault time, and VFD output frequency at fault time.

6.2  Motor Frequency Fault — Diagnostic Tree (E17)

IMPORTANT: On the CP-300 series, E17 is a MOTOR FREQUENCY FAULT — it indicates
that the VFD output frequency deviated from the commanded setpoint by more than
±2 Hz for more than 3 continuous seconds.

E17 on CP-300 IS NOT A TEMPERATURE FAULT.
E17 on CP-300 IS NOT RELATED TO DISCHARGE TEMPERATURE.
E17 on CP-300 IS NOT RELATED TO THE CONDENSER COILS OR COOLING FAN.

(If a technician familiar with CP-200 units sees E17 and checks the condenser
coils, they will find no issue — because E17 on CP-300 is a VFD frequency fault,
not a thermal fault. This is a known cross-training error — see company Tech
Bulletin TN-2023-42 regarding model cross-training requirements.)

Trigger Condition: VFD output frequency deviates >±2 Hz from setpoint for >3 seconds.

Probable Causes (in order of frequency):
  A. VFD firmware bug (units with serial < CP3-202305-0500; see Bulletin BULL-2023-008)
  B. 3-phase input voltage instability (voltage sag, swell, or phase loss)
  C. Incorrect VFD parameter P1.02 (maximum frequency) set too low
  D. VFD input frequency deviation (supply not stable at 60 Hz)
  E. Mechanical overload causing VFD to lose speed regulation
  F. Motor winding fault causing excessive slip
  G. VFD output cable insulation failure (partial discharge)
  H. Faulty VFD frequency sensor/feedback circuit

Diagnostic Steps for E17 on CP-300:
  Step 1: Check VFD firmware version (HMI → About → VFD Firmware)
          If version < 3.2.1 AND serial number < 30500, update firmware immediately
          per Bulletin BULL-2023-008. This is the most common cause of E17 on
          early CP-300 units.

  Step 2: Check VFD parameter P1.01 (base frequency): must match supply frequency
          (60 Hz in North America). Incorrect setting causes persistent E17 faults.
          Access: HMI → Settings → VFD Parameters → P1.01

  Step 3: Verify 3-phase input voltage quality
          - Measure L1, L2, L3 at VFD input terminals with true-RMS meter
          - All three phases must be 460V ±5% (437–483V)
          - Phase-to-phase imbalance must be <2%
          - Verify supply frequency is 60 Hz ±0.5 Hz

  Step 4: Verify VFD output frequency
          - Connect calibrated power meter to VFD output (between VFD and motor)
          - Compare actual output frequency to HMI-displayed setpoint
          - If output frequency matches setpoint but E17 still fires, suspect
            internal VFD feedback circuit fault — replace VFD

  Step 5: Inspect motor
          - Measure motor winding resistance (all three phases within 5% of each other)
          - Measure insulation resistance (>100 MΩ phase-to-ground with megohmmeter)
          - Inspect motor shaft for mechanical overload (seized bearing, coupling fault)

  Step 6: If all above checks pass, contact IndustrialWorks technical support
          with the VFD event log (export via HMI → Faults → Export to USB)

Restart Procedure after E17 on CP-300:
  1. Confirm root cause has been identified and corrected
  2. Reset VFD: HMI → Faults → Reset VFD (or press physical RESET button on VFD door)
  3. Verify VFD DC bus light goes OFF, then back ON as VFD re-initializes
  4. HMI returns to "VFD READY" state
  5. Press START; monitor VFD output frequency on HMI during soft-start
  6. Verify frequency ramps smoothly from 20 Hz to operating frequency
  7. If E17 immediately re-triggers during startup, do NOT retry more than 3 times
     — contact technical support

6.3  Vibration Fault — Diagnostic Tree (E08)

IMPORTANT: On CP-300 series, E08 = VIBRATION FAULT.
(Note: On CP-200 series, E08 = LOW SUCTION PRESSURE — completely different meaning.)

Trigger Condition: Vibration sensor reading exceeds 7.1 mm/s RMS.

Probable Causes:
  A. Worn or damaged screw element bearings
  B. Debris ingestion into screw element (inspect inlet filter)
  C. Oil injection system malfunction (inadequate lubrication causes metal contact)
  D. Coupling failure between motor and screw element
  E. Imbalanced rotor (requires factory inspection)
  F. Foundation resonance or loose mounting bolts

Diagnostic Steps:
  1. Check HMI for vibration trend (HMI → Data → Vibration Log)
  2. Inspect inlet air filter — look for evidence of debris bypass
  3. Verify oil injection rate (should be 8–12 GPM at operating temp)
  4. Measure vibration at motor end bearing and compressor end bearing separately
  5. Tighten all mounting bolts to specified torque (55 ft-lb for M12 isolator bolts)
  6. If vibration is >4 mm/s at bearings, remove from service for bearing inspection

7.3  Detailed Fault Description: E17 — MOTOR FREQUENCY FAULT

Fault Code:        E17
Fault Name:        MOTOR_FREQUENCY_FAULT
Model Applicability: CP-300, CP-301, CP-302 (ALL CP-300 SERIES)
Fault Category:    VFD (Variable Frequency Drive)

=====================================================================
!! CRITICAL DISTINCTION FROM CP-200 SERIES !!
On the CP-200 series, E17 = HIGH DISCHARGE TEMPERATURE (thermal fault).
On the CP-300 series, E17 = MOTOR FREQUENCY FAULT (VFD fault).
These are COMPLETELY DIFFERENT fault conditions requiring different responses.
Checking condenser coils or coolant level will NOT resolve E17 on a CP-300.
=====================================================================

Trigger Condition:
  The VFD continuously monitors its own output frequency and compares it to the
  commanded frequency setpoint from the IW-CTRL-5000 capacity controller. If
  the actual output frequency deviates from the setpoint by more than ±2 Hz for
  more than 3 continuous seconds, the controller latches E17 and commands an
  immediate controlled stop of the VFD (coast-to-stop mode).

Common Scenario:
  During load transitions (e.g., when system demand drops sharply and VFD
  attempts to ramp down frequency rapidly), early-firmware units may fail to
  track the setpoint within the ±2 Hz tolerance. This is the basis of the
  firmware bug described in Bulletin BULL-2023-008.

Immediate Action Required:
  1. Note the VFD output frequency at fault time (recorded in HMI fault log)
  2. Note the commanded setpoint frequency at fault time
  3. Calculate the deviation — if it matches a load-transition scenario, suspect
     firmware bug (check serial number and firmware version first)
  4. Do NOT check condenser coils — this is not a temperature fault
  5. Do NOT check cooling water levels — this is not a cooling fault
  6. Check VFD parameter settings, verify 3-phase input power quality
  7. Inspect motor windings if electrical cause is suspected

Actions:
  A. Check VFD firmware: HMI → About → VFD Firmware
     - Version 3.2.1 or later: firmware is not the issue; proceed to Step B
     - Version < 3.2.1 AND serial < 30500: perform firmware update (Bulletin BULL-2023-008)

  B. Verify VFD parameter P1.01 = 60 (base frequency matches supply)
     Access: HMI → Settings → VFD Parameters → P1.01

  C. Verify 3-phase input voltage stability
     - Connect power quality analyzer to VFD input for minimum 1 hour
     - Acceptable: 460V ±5%, <2% imbalance, 60 Hz ±0.5 Hz
     - If voltage is unstable, address supply-side power quality first

  D. Inspect motor winding insulation (megohmmeter test >100 MΩ)
  E. Check for mechanical overload: verify screw element turns freely

Restart Procedure after E17 on CP-300:
  1. Verify root cause corrected (firmware updated, parameters set, power verified)
  2. Perform VFD reset: HMI → Faults → Reset VFD (latch must clear)
  3. Confirm "VFD READY" status on HMI
  4. Initiate soft-start by pressing START
  5. Watch VFD output frequency on HMI trend screen during ramp-up
  6. Frequency should ramp from 20 Hz → 45 Hz over 15 seconds without deviation
  7. Monitor for E17 recurrence during first load-transition cycle
  8. Log all findings in maintenance record

7.4  Detailed Fault Description: E08 — VIBRATION FAULT

Fault Code:        E08
Fault Name:        VIBRATION_FAULT
Trigger Condition: Vibration sensor reading >7.1 mm/s RMS for >5 seconds.

IMPORTANT: On CP-200 series, E08 = LOW SUCTION PRESSURE.
On CP-300 series, E08 = VIBRATION FAULT. Do not cross-reference.

Root Causes:
  - Worn screw element bearings (most common after 10,000 hours)
  - Debris ingestion past inlet filter
  - Low oil injection causing metal-to-rotor contact and vibration
  - Motor shaft coupling damage or misalignment
  - Imbalanced screw rotors (rotor replacement needed)
  - Loose mounting bolts or degraded vibration isolators
Immediate Action:
  1. Stop unit immediately — continued operation with high vibration will
     rapidly destroy screw element bearings (catastrophic failure within hours)
  2. Inspect oil injection system — confirm oil flow to element
  3. Inspect inlet filter for breach or debris bypass
  4. Measure vibration at both bearing housings
  5. Contact IndustrialWorks service if bearing vibration >4 mm/s
