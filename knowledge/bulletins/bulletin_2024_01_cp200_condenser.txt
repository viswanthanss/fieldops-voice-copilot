================================================================================
TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN: TSB-2024-01
Subject: CP-200 Series Condenser Coil Cleaning & E17 Mitigation
Applies To: All CP-200, CP-201, CP-202 Units Installed in Dusty Environments
Date: January 20, 2024 | Revision: 1
Author: IndustrialWorks Product Reliability Group
================================================================================

1. BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Field monitoring data indicates an increased frequency of Error E17 (High Discharge
Temperature) trips on CP-200 series compressors operating in heavy industrial and
unfiltered manufacturing plant floors. In 82% of reported cases, the primary root
cause was found to be micro-dust compaction within the condenser coil fins, reducing
effective convective heat transfer area by up to 45%.

2. SYMPTOMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Intermittent E17 faults occurring during peak afternoon shifts or high ambient (>90°F) conditions.
- Compressor runs normally unloaded, but discharge temperature escalates rapidly upon 100% step-load.
- Cooling fan is operating at full RPM, but airflow across the discharge plenum is noticeably reduced.

3. CORRECTIVE ACTION & ENHANCED CLEANING PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard dry wiping of external fins is insufficient because particulate penetrates
deep into the staggered fin matrix.

Recommended Cleaning Procedure:
1. Isolate main power (LOTO). Ensure unit is cooled below 100°F.
2. Remove fan guard FG-200 and swing the fan cowl outward.
3. Apply non-acidic aluminum coil cleaner (IW-CLN-902) using a low-pressure pump sprayer.
   Allow 8–10 minutes dwell time for chemical breakdown of oil/dust cake.
4. Back-flush the coil from the inside out using dry compressed air regulated to
   maximum 35 PSIG (to avoid bending thin aluminum fins) or low-pressure warm water.
5. Straighten any bent or crushed fins using fin comb tool FC-14 (14 fins/inch).
6. Reassemble fan guard and verify blade tip clearance (minimum 0.25 inch / 6.3 mm).

4. PREVENTATIVE MAINTENANCE UPDATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For units operating at Site A, the inspection frequency of condenser assembly CP200-RAD-01
is hereby revised from 500 hours to 250 hours. Technicians must record static differential
pressure across the coil at every bi-weekly PM round.
