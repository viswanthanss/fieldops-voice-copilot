================================================================================
TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN: TSB-2023-08
Subject: CP-300 Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) Parameter Calibration for Error E17
Applies To: All CP-300, CP-301, CP-302 Variable Speed Rotary Screw Units
Date: August 14, 2023 | Revision: 2
Author: IndustrialWorks Electrical & Drive Systems Engineering
================================================================================

1. CRITICAL MODEL ARCHITECTURE DISTINCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTICE TO ALL TECHNICIANS:
Error E17 on the CP-300 platform signifies MOTOR FREQUENCY FAULT (VFD slip/hunting).
This is entirely distinct from older CP-200 reciprocating models where E17 designates
discharge temperature. DO NOT perform thermal or condenser coil troubleshooting
for an E17 alarm on a CP-300 unit.

2. ROOT CAUSE & DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On CP-300 rotary screw compressors, the main drive motor is controlled by an
integrated 460V variable frequency inverter (Drive Model VFD-INV-300).
Error code E17 trips when the measured output frequency deviates from the command
reference frequency by more than 4.5 Hz for 500 milliseconds, or when the drive
detects phase angle instability during load modulation between 30 Hz and 60 Hz.

Primary causes:
- Loose encoder feedback cable shielding causing EMF noise interference.
- Inadequate acceleration/deceleration ramp rates under rapid plant air demand swings.
- Motor slip compensation parameter (Parameter P-402) out of factory tolerance.

3. FIELD RECTIFICATION PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Inverter Lockout & Isolation:
        Ensure drive DC bus capacitor voltage has discharged to < 24V DC before
        opening drive cabinet enclosure door (wait minimum 10 minutes after LOTO).

Step 2: Encoder & Grounding Inspection:
        Inspect quadrature encoder cable connected to Terminal Block TB-4.
        Verify ground braid is terminated to chassis star ground with < 0.1 ohm resistance.
        Retighten terminal screws to 4.4 in-lbs (0.5 Nm).

Step 3: Drive Parameter Verification (via Keypad Programmer):
        Navigate to Parameter Group 4 (Motor Control):
        - Parameter P-401 (Base Frequency): Verify set to exactly 60.0 Hz.
        - Parameter P-402 (Slip Compensation): Adjust from default 1.2% to 0.8%.
        - Parameter P-405 (Current Limit): Ensure set to 110% of FLA (Full Load Amps).
        - Parameter P-108 (Accel Ramp Time): Set to 12.0 seconds (do not set below 10.0s).

Step 4: Calibration Run:
        Energize control power.
        Execute static auto-tune sequence: Menu -> Calibration -> VFD Motor Identification.
        Verify drive displays "TUNE OK". Clear E17 fault from memory.
        Operate compressor in manual speed sweep from 1,800 RPM to 3,600 RPM.
